Memory Lane

A concept brand for a safe and guided day-trip service meant for seniors living with Alzheimer's Disease.

Memory Lane provides affordable, guided local outings for seniors with Alzheimer's across the Lower Mainland. Through thoughtfully planned experiences, we support their physical and cognitive well-being while bringing more connection and enrichment into their everyday lives.

I created all the illustration assets for this branding project, in collaboration with Charis Catotocan, who was in charge of the branding, typography and layouts.

To invoke a sense of nostalgia and calm, I chose to use a hand-drawn, pen and ink inspired style. I wanted to mimic the black and white cartoons that people grew up with in the 60s-70s, which means keeping my linework loose and textured.

Each character has a different personality being shown. Some seniors look younger than others, because Alzheimer's Disease can affect individuals of all ages. People using this service will come from all kinds of personalities and backgrounds.
