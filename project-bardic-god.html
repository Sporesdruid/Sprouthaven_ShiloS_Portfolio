The Bardic God

Garden Street Ghost Agency

The Bardic God is a character I developed over high school. He is very near and dear to my heart, so I enjoy making videos exploring his backstory!

Milo "Feather" Oliver was once a destitute boy, now masquerading as a winged God with the help of his sister Clover and a shapeshifting entity named El.

The music in the video is Harpy Hare by Yaelokre. The video is not monetized.
